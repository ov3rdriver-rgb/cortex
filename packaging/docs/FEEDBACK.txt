CORTEX {{VERSION}} - HOW TO SEND A REPORT
===================================

Send your reports to the person who gave you this package.


1. BEFORE YOU SEND A REPORT
---------------------------
 1. Read KNOWN-ISSUES.txt.
 2. Make sure that Cortex shows "v{{VERSION}} ({{HASH}})" at the top right
    of its window.


2. PROBLEM REPORT
-----------------
Give this data in each problem report:

 1. The version that Cortex shows.
 2. The plug-in: Cortex (effect) or Cortex Instrument.
 3. The format: VST3 or stand-alone.
 4. Your DAW and its version.
 5. Your version of Windows.
 6. The sample rate and the buffer size of your audio device.
 7. The approximate number of blocks in each brain.
 8. The steps that cause the problem. Write one action in each step.
 9. What you expected.
10. What occurred.

If possible, also send:
 - A screenshot of the Cortex window.
 - A short audio recording of the problem.

Example of steps:
  1. Put Cortex on a drum track.
  2. Load a folder of 40 piano samples into Brain 1.
  3. Set Low Latency to "on".
  4. Play the track.
  Result: The sound stops after 2 seconds.


3. IF THE DAW STOPS (CRASH)
---------------------------
 1. Write down what you did immediately before the crash.
 2. If the DAW makes a crash report, keep the report.
 3. Send the report and the steps.


4. GENERAL COMMENTS
-------------------
We also want your comments. For example:
 - Sounds that you like.
 - Controls that are difficult to understand.
 - Functions that you want.
