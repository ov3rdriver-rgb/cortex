CORTEX {{VERSION}} - INSTALLATION (WINDOWS)
=====================================

1. INSTALL THE VST3 PLUG-INS
----------------------------
CAUTION: CLOSE YOUR DAW BEFORE YOU DO THIS PROCEDURE. IF THE DAW IS
OPEN, WINDOWS CANNOT REPLACE A PLUG-IN THAT THE DAW USES.

 1. Close your DAW.
 2. Open the VST3 folder in this package.
 3. Select "Cortex.vst3" and "Cortex Instrument.vst3".
 4. Copy the two items.
 5. Open this folder:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
 6. Paste the two items into the folder.
 7. If Windows asks for administrator permission, click "Continue".
 8. If Windows asks to replace items, click "Replace".
 9. Start your DAW.
10. Tell the DAW to scan for new plug-ins. Refer to the manual of
    your DAW.
11. Find "Cortex" in the list of effects.
12. Find "Cortex Instrument" in the list of instruments.


2. MAKE SURE THAT THE CORRECT VERSION IS INSTALLED
--------------------------------------------------
 1. Put Cortex on a track.
 2. Open the Cortex window.
 3. Look at the top right corner of the window.
 4. Make sure that the text is "v{{VERSION}} ({{HASH}})".

If the text is different, your DAW uses an older copy of Cortex.
Do the procedure in section 1 again.


3. USE THE STAND-ALONE PROGRAMS
-------------------------------
You can use Cortex without a DAW.

 1. Open the Standalone folder in this package.
 2. Copy the folder to a location on your computer.
 3. Double-click "Cortex.exe" or "Cortex Instrument.exe".
 4. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info".
 5. Click "Run anyway".
 6. Click "Options".
 7. Select your audio device.
 8. For Cortex Instrument, select your MIDI input device.

NOTE: Windows shows the warning in step 4 because the programs do
not have a digital signature.


4. REMOVE CORTEX
----------------
 1. Close your DAW.
 2. Open this folder:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
 3. Delete "Cortex.vst3" and "Cortex Instrument.vst3".
 4. Delete the stand-alone programs, if you copied them.

Cortex keeps a cache of analysed brains in this folder:
      %APPDATA%\Cortex\BrainCache
You can delete this folder. The cache uses a maximum of 3 GB.
