CORTEX {{VERSION}} - KNOWN ISSUES
===========================

Do not send reports about these issues. We know about them.


1. TESTS NOT DONE
-----------------
 - We did not test Cortex in Ableton Live, Logic Pro or Reaper.
   Please tell us how Cortex works in your DAW.
 - This package does not contain a version for macOS.


2. SOUND
--------
 - When you move a dot in a triangle, the volume can change.
   Use "Output Gain" in the Output tab to adjust the volume.
 - With Low Latency on, each note starts with the sound of the Input.
   The colour of the brains comes approximately 20 ms later.
 - With Low Latency on, low notes can sound rough.
 - If the Input is a sidechain, and the Envelope or Detail triangle
   uses Brain 2, the sidechain and Brain 2 are not aligned in time.


3. BRAINS
---------
 - A large folder of samples takes time to analyse. A folder with
   150,000 blocks can take several minutes. Cortex shows the progress
   in the brain column.
 - When you change "Block Size" or "Block Overlap", Cortex analyses
   the brains again.
 - The brain map can look full with very large brains.


4. PROJECTS
-----------
 - Projects that you saved with a version before 1.0.0 open with the
   morph controls at their default positions. The brain folders load
   correctly.
 - The delay of Cortex changes when you load a brain or change the FFT
   size. Some DAWs only adjust to the new delay when you stop and
   start playback.


5. WINDOW
---------
 - The minimum size of the window is 1120 x 760 pixels. On a small
   screen, the window can be larger than the screen.


6. INSTALLATION
---------------
 - The programs do not have a digital signature. Windows can show a
   warning when you start a stand-alone program. Refer to INSTALL.txt,
   section 3.
