CORTEX {{VERSION}} - QUICK START
==========================

1. THE CORTEX WINDOW
--------------------
LEFT COLUMN      Brain 1 (orange).
RIGHT COLUMN     Brain 2 (violet).
CENTRE, TOP      Two triangles: "Envelope" and "Detail".
CENTRE, MIDDLE   The spectral envelopes of the sources.
CENTRE, BOTTOM   The cepstrum and the cutoff lines.
BOTTOM, LEFT     The Input panel (turquoise).
BOTTOM, RIGHT    Tabs: "Search", "Mod" and "Output".

Each triangle has one source at each corner:
 - Brain 1 is at the top.
 - Brain 2 is at the bottom left.
 - Input is at the bottom right.


2. MAKE A FIRST SOUND (EFFECT)
------------------------------
 1. Put Cortex on an audio track that has a sound.
 2. Open the Cortex window.
 3. Drag a folder of samples onto the Brain 1 column.
 4. Wait until the column shows the number of blocks.
    NOTE: A large folder can take some minutes the first time.
    Cortex then keeps the analysis in a cache.
 5. Play the track.
 6. Drag the dot in the Envelope triangle to different positions.
 7. Listen to the change in the colour of the sound.

With the default settings, the envelope comes from Brain 1 and the
detail comes from the Input. Thus, the sound has the notes of the
track and the colour of the samples in Brain 1.


3. MAKE A FIRST SOUND (INSTRUMENT)
----------------------------------
 1. Put Cortex Instrument on an instrument track.
 2. Open the Cortex window.
 3. Drag a folder of samples onto the Brain 1 column.
 4. Wait until the column shows the number of blocks.
 5. Play MIDI notes.
 6. Drag the dot in the Envelope triangle.


4. USE TWO BRAINS
-----------------
 1. Drag a folder of samples onto the Brain 1 column.
 2. Drag a different folder onto the Brain 2 column.
 3. In the Envelope triangle, drag the dot towards Brain 2.
 4. In the Detail triangle, drag the dot towards Brain 1.
 5. Play the Input.

Example: put music in Brain 1 and drum breaks in Brain 2. The result
has the notes and texture of the music and the colour of the drums.

Each corner shows a percentage. This is the part of the mix that
comes from that source.

NOTE: If a brain is empty, its corner shows 0%. The Input then
gives that part of the mix.


5. OTHER CONTROLS
-----------------
LINK                 When "Link" is on, the Detail triangle follows
                     the Envelope triangle.
CUTOFF LINES         Drag a line in the cepstrum to change where the
                     envelope of that source stops and the detail
                     starts.
BRAIN 1 SEARCH,      Each brain has its own Search tab. The settings
BRAIN 2 SEARCH       in a tab apply to that brain only.
  CHAOS              Increase Chaos to make the brain change blocks
                     more frequently.
  MEMORY             Increase Memory to make the brain play blocks
                     in the order of its samples.
  MATCH TIMBRE       Increase to make the brain match the colour of
                     the Input.
  MATCH PITCH        Increase to make the brain match the notes of
                     the Input.
LINK RINGS           The two small rings in the corner of a brain
                     control. Click them to link that control between
                     the brains: when you turn it on one brain, the
                     other brain follows. "Link all" at the right of
                     the tabs links every brain control.
BLOCK SIZE, OVERLAP  In each brain column. The length of the blocks
                     of that brain, and how much they overlap. Each
                     brain has its own. When you change them, Cortex
                     analyses that brain again.
CAPTURE              Cortex always keeps the last 2 minutes of its
                     output. Click "Capture 60 s" in the header to
                     save the last 60 seconds as a WAV file in your
                     Music folder, in "Cortex Captures". Then drag the
                     file name that appears into your DAW. Set the
                     number of seconds with "Capture Length" in the
                     Output tab.
OUTPUT, LOW LATENCY  Set to "on" when you play live. The delay
                     decreases from approximately 85 ms to
                     approximately 11 ms.
OUTPUT, THEME        "Dark" or "Light". Your choice applies to every
                     Cortex you open. Projects do not change it.
IN / OUT METERS      The header shows the Input level, the output
                     level and a light that is on when the gate is
                     open.

To set a triangle back to its default position, double-click the
triangle.
